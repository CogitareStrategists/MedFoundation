About TRICON 2026

TRICON 2026 brings together the 10th Triennial Conference of the Academy of Clinical Microbiologists with pre-conference workshops and CME programmes in Hyderabad. Delegates can review the detailed programme, faculty, registration information, venue guidance, and accommodation references on the conference page.
