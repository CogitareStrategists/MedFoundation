About the conference

TRICON 2026 is being organised by the Medical & Health Care Foundation in association with the Academy of Clinical Microbiologists and Rainbow Children’s Hospital, Hyderabad. The conference runs from 7th to 9th August 2026, with pre-conference workshops and CME programmes on 5th and 6th August 2026.

The event includes scientific sessions, keynote lectures, parallel symposia, oral and poster paper presentations, and focused sessions relevant to clinical microbiology, infection control, public health, and antimicrobial stewardship.

Important dates

5–6 Aug 2026 Pre-conference workshops / CME
Workshops and focused CME sessions take place before the main conference.
7–9 Aug 2026 Main conference
Scientific sessions, symposia, orations, keynotes, faculty talks, and paper presentations.
30 Jun 2026 Abstract submission deadline
Abstracts are to be submitted as per the brochure instructions.
