About the conference

TRICON 2026 is being organised by the Medical & Health Care Foundation in association with the Academy of Clinical Microbiologists and Rainbow Children’s Hospital, Hyderabad. The conference runs from 7th to 9th August 2026, with pre-conference workshops and CME programmes on 5th and 6th August 2026.

The event includes scientific sessions, keynote lectures, parallel symposia, oral and poster paper presentations, and focused sessions relevant to clinical microbiology, infection control, public health, and antimicrobial stewardship.

Important dates

5–6 Aug 2026 Pre-conference workshops / CME
Workshops and focused CME sessions take place before the main conference.
7–9 Aug 2026 Main conference
Scientific sessions, symposia, orations, keynotes, faculty talks, and paper presentations.
30 Jun 2026 Abstract submission deadline
Abstracts are to be submitted as per the brochure instructions.

Detailed programme

The detailed day-wise programme is provided below, with the brochure available for the complete official schedule and notes.

Open Brochure PDF
5–6 August 2026 CME – Advancing the Fight against Tuberculosis
Focus: advancing the fight against tuberculosis through laboratory diagnosis.
6 August 2026 Workshop – ABC of ANA: Decoding ANA
Designed to make ANA interpretation simple, structured, and clinically meaningful for clinical microbiologists.
5–6 August 2026 Workshop – Practical Examination: A Clinically Relevant One
Clinical microbiology workshop focused on examination patterns relevant to current practice.
Morning Inauguration, Presidential Oration and ACM Oration
Conference inauguration followed by presidential oration and the ACM oration.
Symposium 1 Viral Infections and Atypical Pneumonia
Influenza over the last two years, mycoplasma infections in 2025, and viral gastroenteritis overview.
Symposium 2 Tropical Infections
Antimicrobial resistance and enteric fever, dengue fever experience, and ocular infections in the tropics.
Afternoon Keynote Address, Free Oral Paper and Poster Sessions
Keynote session followed by paper presentation sessions and general body meeting.
Symposium 3 & 4 Bone and Joint Infections / Critical Care Infections
Diagnosis and management of prosthetic joint infections, childhood bone and joint infections, rapid microbiology in critical care, and infection control bundles.
Morning Prof Mabel Legori Memorial Quiz
Quiz session before the scientific symposia begin.
Symposium 5 & 6 Mycobacterial Infections / Skin and Soft Tissue Infections
TBM management in children, diagnosis of tuberculosis, NTM infections and SSTI, and diabetic foot infections.
Keynote and Symposium 7 & 8 How Do I? / Concerns of the Day
Addressing AMR, interpreting ECOFFs, BL/BLI challenges, RAST usefulness, AI in infection control, BSI and CVC related infections in HSCT, and ethical considerations in patient isolation.
Afternoon ACM Function and Lunch
ACM function followed by lunch and continuation of the scientific programme.
Symposium 9, 10 & 11 Antibiogram, Hospital Systems, AMS & OPAT
Clinically relevant antibiogram, antibiotic policy, water management in hospitals, HVAC in hospitals, diagnostic stewardship, outpatient AMS, and antifungal stewardship.
Morning Special Lectures – Hot Topics
Implementing respiratory molecular panels, preventing ECMO infections, and emerging infectious agents in blood transfusion.
Public Health & Infection Control Practical Applications and Interactive Case Discussions
Public health microbiology, epidemiological markers, and interactive real cases in hospital and community outbreaks.
Closing session Valedictory Function
Conference valedictory function followed by lunch and disperse.
