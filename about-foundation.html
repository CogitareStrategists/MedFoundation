Foundation overview

The Medical & Health Care Foundation presents itself as an academic and educational platform with a strong interest in clinical microbiology, infection prevention, and professional development for healthcare workers and doctors.

Through conferences, workshops, CME programmes, forums, and training-oriented activities, the foundation supports learning, dissemination of good practices, and academic exchange in medicine and laboratory-related disciplines.

Areas of work

  • Teaching and dissemination of knowledge in clinical microbiology and related medical disciplines
  • Awareness of infection prevention practices in hospitals and the community
  • Educational forums, training centres, workshops, and CME programmes
  • Support for young doctors, postgraduate learning, and academic development
  • Academic support for pathology and laboratory medicine related activities

TRICON 2026 and the foundation

TRICON 2026 is being organised by the Medical & Health Care Foundation in association with the Academy of Clinical Microbiologists and Rainbow Children’s Hospital, Hyderabad. On this website, TRICON 2026 is the featured conference, while the foundation remains the organising body behind the event.
